# Four Haiku

*Small tributes, one for each thing built in this folder.*

---

### for the writings

slow afternoon light
across paper that wasn't there
when I started typing

---

### for the constellation

a hundred and seventy-one
small lamps held up
on the same dark hill

---

### for the boids

the field at dusk —
the geese decide together
without deciding

---

### for the garden

the seed I planted
yesterday: a small green claim
on the same square inch
