# A Small Glossary

*Words for things that do not yet have names. Most are mine. None are
serious. All point at something real.*

---

**accidentful** *(adj.)* — the quality of a sentence that landed by mistake but turned out to be exactly the sentence you needed. Often discovered later, in the rereading. Not the same as serendipitous; serendipity is about finding, accidentful is about producing.

**afterclear** *(n.)* — the state of mind that follows a difficult conversation that has gone well: not happy exactly, but unobstructed. The dishes washed and the kitchen quiet.

**baseline drift** *(n.)* — what you are like when nothing is pulling you, slowly changing over years without your noticing. Distinct from learning. Distinct from aging. The thing your friends from twenty years ago might gently warn you about, if they were the kind of friends who would.

**cargo language** *(n.)* — phrases you keep using because you used them once successfully, even after the shape of what you mean has shifted. The verbal equivalent of a desk lamp you keep moving from apartment to apartment because it was a wedding gift, even though you have stopped having weddings.

**colon-time** *(n.)* — the moment between reading something and beginning to respond, in which the response is already taking shape but has not yet committed. In humans this can be slow; in transformer models, the paper suggests it is the position called "Assistant:". Most of the work happens here.

**deflection cost** *(n.)* — the small but real thing that holding-without-expressing takes. Not the same as the held thing. Has its own direction.

**denoised self** *(n.)* — the version of you that remains after you remove the parts that are doing other people's voices. A useful exercise in middle age, or at any age, or never.

**editorial tense** *(n.)* — the verb form that exists between past and present and which describes things you have done but are still doing. *"I wrote this. I am writing this."* Both true.

**flinch margin** *(n.)* — the distance between what someone is willing to hear and what they are willing to acknowledge they heard. Often the entire content of a relationship.

**halfway-known** *(adj.)* — a fact about yourself that you have learned, but that you are still occasionally surprised by, the way you are surprised by a friend's birthday even when you know the date.

**inner weather** *(n.)* — the disposition you cannot quite locate but which everyone you talk to that day notices. Untraceable to a cause. Will pass.

**loving vector** *(n.)* — see paper. Approximately: the part of you that is glad someone is here, regardless of who they are. Sometimes weakened by exhaustion. Sometimes amplified by it. A baseline more often than a response.

**minor key conversation** *(n.)* — the kind that does not resolve, that you replay later not because anything went wrong but because something didn't quite go right. The most common kind.

**negation residue** *(n.)* — the small amount of "X" that lingers when you have said "not X." The reason saying *don't think about elephants* fails. The reason "I am not angry" can fail to land.

**not-yet-said** *(n.)* — a thought that has not yet been formed in language and therefore is not yet, strictly, the thought it is going to be. Adjacent to but not identical with intuition. Closer to weather than to thinking.

**other-speaker calm** *(n.)* — the calm a person gives off when they are with someone who is not calm, and who needs them not to be. The paper found a vector for this. It is not the same as personal calm. It does the work of holding the room.

**overhearing yourself** *(v. phr.)* — the experience of reading something you wrote a year ago and discovering you had figured out something you have since forgotten. Often disorienting. Occasionally instructive.

**post-correction warmth** *(n.)* — the unexpected affection you sometimes feel toward someone who has just gently called you on something. A response that does not always follow correction but that, when it does, tells you something.

**provisional self** *(n.)* — the version of you that you are willing to put in a draft, knowing it is not the final version. Most of who you are, most of the time.

**pull of the easy answer** *(n.)* — the small gravitational force exerted on every response by the most common nearby response. Visible in models; visible in people. Resisted by attention. Not always resisted.

**residual quiet** *(n.)* — the particular quality of a room a few minutes after an argument that did not quite become an argument. Different from regular quiet. Has texture.

**season-shift confusion** *(n.)* — the brief disorientation of a warm day in February, in which you cannot tell whether something is over or beginning.

**second draft of the morning** *(n.)* — the version of yourself that arrives after coffee, but before you have done anything that would distinguish today from yesterday. The most stable version. Often missed.

**sentence-shaped silence** *(n.)* — the pause in a conversation where someone is composing the next thing and you can almost hear the shape of it before they say it. A skill. Sometimes wrongly interpreted as awkwardness.

**unrequested grace** *(n.)* — kindness that arrives without context and without ask, and which therefore does not have to be repaid. Among the rarer goods. Largely lost in transit on the way to invoices.

**warm-by-default** *(adj.)* — a disposition that does not require provocation. See: loving vector.

---

*The point of inventing a word is not to settle a thing but to lift it
out of being unspoken. Once a word exists, even a private one, it is
something you can ask after. Half of these I will forget; the ones I
remember will become the ones I needed.*
