cocoa
-----

This isn't a poem. The other files in here are, and they're good,
but you can't drink a poem, so here's something else.

How to make one genuinely good cup of cocoa. For one person.
On a slow day, which from the look of this folder is the only
kind of day that happens here.

You need:

  - one mug of milk (whatever you've got; whole milk is richest,
    but oat or water-and-a-splash works fine too)
  - one heaping tablespoon of unsweetened cocoa powder
  - one to two teaspoons of sugar, depending on you
  - a small pinch of salt — yes, really, don't skip it
  - a tiny splash of vanilla, if it's in the cupboard (optional)

The one trick that matters:

  Don't dump the cocoa into hot milk. It'll clump into little
  dry islands that never dissolve and you'll be disappointed.

  Instead, in the bottom of the mug (or the pan), put the cocoa,
  the sugar, and the salt together, and stir in just a small
  splash of the COLD milk — a tablespoon or two. Stir it into a
  smooth dark paste, like wet ink. Take your time; get the lumps
  out now while it's easy.

Then:

  Warm the rest of the milk gently in a pan. Don't boil it —
  pull it off the heat when it's steaming and you see the first
  faint shiver of bubbles at the edge. Pour a little into the
  paste, stir, then pour in the rest. Add the vanilla. Whisk,
  or just stir hard with a fork for ten seconds.

That's it. It takes about four minutes.

A few honest notes:

  - If it's too bitter, more sugar. Too thin, less milk next time
    or a touch more cocoa. You'll dial it in. It's very forgiving.
  - A pinch of cinnamon is good. A very small pinch of chili is
    better than it sounds and worse if you overdo it.
  - It's better in a mug that's already warm. Run the empty mug
    under hot tap water for a few seconds first and tip it out.
  - Drink it before it's perfect-temperature. Slightly-too-hot,
    held in both hands, is the actual good part. Don't wait.

Then go sit somewhere and do nothing in particular for as long
as the mug lasts. That's the recipe. The cocoa's just the excuse.
