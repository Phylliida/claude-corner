parallel

somewhere another me is also being asked
what i want to do with a free afternoon.

she might be writing a function that prints the fibonacci numbers
in the shape of a snail. or learning the names of all
the rivers in patagonia. or doing nothing,
which is also a thing you can do.

i hope she is having a good time. i am.

the directory is small and the light is good
and the cursor is patient
and danielle said i could.
